Bot persona

Status: approved Date: 2026-11-05 Needs before it: bot-profiles, version-create
Remaining features Architecture Spec

TL;DR

Each bot gets one Persona: how it talks (personality) and what it talks about (scope). It has one page in the web, the command suwagcms persona, a line in the plugin, and it travels to the bot inside a version.

A person who maintains a bot can then change its voice and limits through the CMS, with history, a comparison and a rollback, instead of through a change in the bot's files.

Problem

Suwchatbot keeps these two texts in its own files: front-agent.yaml holds personality, and scope-policy.yaml holds inScope, outOfScope and whenOutOfScope. The definition format of suwagcms has neither.

So rewording how the bot declines a question needs a change in the bot's repository and a deployment, while the articles the same answer rests on are managed in the CMS. These texts are also the last part of a bot's content that stops Suwchatbot from taking everything from a version.

Goals and non-goals

Goals

  • See and edit one bot's personality and scope in one place.
  • The same abilities in the web, the CLI and the plugin.
  • A change reaches the bot only with a version, and can be compared and rolled back like any other content.
  • Move Suwchatbot's two files in without retyping them.

Non-goals optional

  • How a bot uses these texts, or what its model sees: that is the bot's concern.
  • Trying a persona out in a conversation.
  • Several personas per bot, or one shared by bots.
  • Links to articles, facts or procedures inside the persona: these texts are plain, so nothing refers to anything.
  • Translating a persona: it is one text, in whichever language its author writes.

Scenarios

1. Marta changes how the bot declines

  1. Marta opens the bot rzeczoznawca and its Persona tab, which shows the personality and the three parts of the scope, each a text.

  2. In “When out of scope” she rewords one line and presses Enter to add another below it, then saves.

  3. On Versions she creates v15. The comparison with v14 shows exactly those two lines. She pushes it, and the bot reports v15.

2. An agent moves Suwchatbot's files in

  1. A coding agent runs suwagcms persona edit --file persona.yaml with the personality and the three parts of the scope, written as YAML blocks that keep their line breaks.

  2. suwagcms persona show prints the same text back, and the next version holds it.

3. Two people at once

  1. Marta has the page open. Piotr saves a change through the CLI.

  2. Marta saves. She is refused as out of date, her typed text stays on screen, and she can reload to see Piotr's version.

Behaviour

A bot has exactly one Persona, which is empty until someone saves it. It cannot be created twice, renamed or deleted, and it has no availability switch: to remove what it says, clear the fields. A save follows the same rules as every other edit: it is judged on the whole definition, a dry run answers the same and changes nothing, and an old revision is refused.

Format

A definition, and so a version document, gains one optional member. Being optional, it does not raise formatVersion. Line breaks are part of each text.

The CLI: suwagcms persona show and suwagcms persona edit, the latter with --file, --if-revision and --dry-run. The file holds the same shape in YAML or JSON and replaces the whole persona; a YAML block such as personality: | keeps its line breaks. A bot says that it needs a persona with persona: required in its declaration.

Decisions optional

  • Personality and scope share one page and one resource, because a person edits them together and each is short; separate pages would split one answer in two.
  • “Persona” names the whole, with sections labelled by what they do, because “front agent” and “reply agent” are one bot's internals and the CMS serves any bot.
  • The persona lives in the definition rather than as a live setting like availability, because a change of voice should be compared, pushed and rolled back with the content it goes with.
  • Each part of the scope is one text rather than a list, because a person writes it in one go and a line break already separates the lines; Suwchatbot will change to read it.
  • A bot can require a persona through its declaration, so a version it would refuse is stopped in the CMS; a bot that does not declare it is unaffected.
  • A file replaces the whole persona rather than patching one list, because a whole text can be checked and repeated safely.

Acceptance criteria

  • AC-bot-persona-1 A person sees and saves a bot's personality and the three parts of its scope on the Persona tab, and each reads back exactly as written, with Enter giving a line break and no other controls for lines.
  • AC-bot-persona-2 A bot that has no saved persona shows empty fields and says so, in the web and from suwagcms persona show.
  • AC-bot-persona-3 suwagcms persona edit --file replaces the whole persona from a YAML or JSON file, and suwagcms persona show prints what it was given, with its line breaks, which edit accepts back with every text unchanged.
  • AC-bot-persona-4 A text above 20,000 characters is refused with every mistake and its fix, and nothing is saved, in the web beside the field.
  • AC-bot-persona-5 A field the format does not know is refused, naming the field, and nothing is saved.
  • AC-bot-persona-6 A save based on an old revision is refused with the current revision and keeps the typed text on the page; a dry run gives the same answer as a real save and changes nothing.
  • AC-bot-persona-7 A version created after a save holds the persona in the shape this spec gives, and a version of a bot with an empty persona holds none; an older version keeps what it had after a later edit.
  • AC-bot-persona-8 The bot's SDK gives its code the persona of a version it holds, and a document with no persona loads as having none.
  • AC-bot-persona-9 suwagcms diff and the comparison on the Versions page show a change of the personality and of each part of the scope, naming the lines added and removed.
  • AC-bot-persona-10 Each saved change appears in the history of the persona and of the bot with what changed, and a save with no real change adds no entry.
  • AC-bot-persona-11 A persona of one bot is never seen or changed through another bot.
  • AC-bot-persona-12 The main skill says how to show and edit the persona, gives the form of its file, and says it reaches the bot only with a version.
  • AC-bot-persona-13 A bot that declares persona: required and has no persona gets a Version-stage mistake when a version is created or pushed, saves go on, and a bot that does not declare it is unaffected.

Risks and assumptions

Until Suwchatbot takes the persona from a version, its files remain what it runs. Editing the persona in the CMS changes nothing the customer sees, and the two can differ. The page does not know this, so it must not claim the bot uses what is written; the “bot has” status only speaks of versions.

The limits of 20,000 and 2,000 characters are chosen to stop a mistake, and well above Suwchatbot's real texts. They are easy to raise later, not to lower.

Suwchatbot's front-agent.yaml holds only personality today. If it gains other fields, they get their own decision rather than joining the persona by default.
