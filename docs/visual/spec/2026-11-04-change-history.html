Spec

History of changes

Status: approved Date: 2026-11-04 Branch: feature/one-history-module Feature: 35 of the suwagcms feature map Needs before it: articles, pagination
Remaining features Architecture Spec

TL;DR

Every change to a resource records what changed and when, with the old and new values. The resource's History panel and suwagcms history show it, even after the resource is deleted.

README lets agents change a bot by themselves, and history is part of what makes that safe. The record cannot be edited. It does not yet say who made a change: that waits for cms-identity, listed below as a criterion for later.

One module everywhere. The History page, Persona, every resource and every version draw the same rows, write a change the same way and page the same way. The module holds no name: the page that embeds it says “History”. A version gets its own address, .../versions/14, laid out like Persona: its number, digest and content, with a History panel beneath holding what happened between the previous version and this one. “Changes” and the separate comparison look go away, and Compare with… stays there as the one place with a net result between any two versions.

A change is written as a comparison writes it, with kept and folded lines; a procedure's entries read as parts; a version's window holds every entry; and a version's own pushes are not on its page. suwagcms history --json stays as it is, and suwagcms versions window reads the window.

Problem

A customer gets the wrong price. The version shows the text that went out, and version-diff shows which version changed it, but neither says when the text was changed, what it said in between, or what else changed that morning. Several saves can fall between two versions, and many never reach one, so versions alone cannot answer.

The same question also has two answers on screen today. The History page, Persona and each resource's edit view share one list, while Versions has a look of its own: “Changes” opens a comparison panel under the table, “Open” a panel with the digest and the document, and neither has an address a person can reload or send, because ?open= is taken once and dropped. The two also write a change differently:

HistoryA comparison
a long textremoved and added lines only, trimmed at both ends: two edits 40 lines apart read as those 40 lines all removed and addedaligned line by line, with two kept lines around each change and the rest folded
a short value− 80, then + 120maxLength: 80 → 120
a proceduresteps as two long lines of JSONits fields, then each step and local fact as a part

The module also names itself: “Loading the history…” and “when history started” are inside it, so a page cannot name it in its own way.

Goals and non-goals

Goals

  • For any resource, see each change: when and what.
  • A record that nobody can edit, and that outlives the resource.
  • See recent changes across one bot.
  • One module wherever changes are listed: same rows, same way of writing a change, same pagination.
  • A version has an address and a page laid out like Persona, with what happened between the previous version and this one.

Non-goals optional

  • Saying who made a change: see “Criteria after other features”.
  • Restoring an old value from history: a rollback changes only the bot, and an edit is a new change anyone can make.
  • Recording reads, or refused attempts.
  • Node positions in the creator: moving a node is not a change of a procedure.
  • A note on a change: no one has asked for it.
  • Changing what the CLI prints: suwagcms history and suwagcms diff print what they print today, with their own words and marks.
  • Saying on a version's page when it was pushed (owner's decision): its pushes come after its creation, so they are outside its window, and Recent pushes on Versions, the row's link and suwagcms history version 14 already say it.
  • Pushing from a version's page: the push stays on Versions, where the bot's status is.
  • Paging a comparison: one answer, as long as the resources that differ, read whole.
  • Links on a comparison's rows: an entry's resource links to its History page because that is how the list is narrowed; nothing has asked for it on a comparison, and in the rollback's question a link would leave the question.

Scenarios

1. Marta finds out when a price changed

$ suwagcms history article cennik 2026-11-04 21:40 edited revision 8 content - Wycena domu: 900 zł + Wycena domu: 1100 zł 2026-11-02 09:12 added revision 1

2. A deleted article

  1. stary-cennik is gone, and a customer quotes it. suwagcms history article stary-cennik still shows when it was deleted and what it said last.

3. What happened this morning

  1. On the bot's History page, Marta sees every change to the bot, newest first.

4. A resource older than history

  1. rabaty has not changed since history started. Its panel says no changes have been recorded since the day history started.

5. Marta opens a version

  1. Customers got a wrong price after v14. On Versions she clicks v14 and lands on /bots/rzeczoznawca/versions/14, with its number, digest and content at the top.

  2. The History panel beneath lists what happened after v13 was created, up to v14's creation: cennik edited at 21:40, a batch of three, and v14 created. She sends the address to Marek, and he sees the same.

6. A change made and restored

  1. Between v13 and v14 someone raised a price at 10:00 and restored it at 10:05. The panel shows both entries, since both happened. Compare with… v13 says there are no differences, since nothing differs. Each answers its own question, so both stay.

7. A version older than history

  1. v3 was created before history started. Its page shows its digest and content as any other, and the panel says changes made before that day were not recorded, so the list may be incomplete.

Inside the module no word says history, in any state: while loading it says “Loading…”, and with nothing to show it says “No change has been recorded since 2026-11-04.” Moving between pages is pagination's, with “Older changes” gone and the panel's place under historyPage; nothing here repeats it. A failure shows what the server answered, as it does today.

One way of writing a change

An entry, a comparison and a rollback's “what goes back” draw the same row: a badge with the action, the resource when the page lists several, the time, revision and batch when there are any, and the change below.

The fieldHow it is written
a short valuemaxLength: 80 → 120, with (none) for a side that has no value
content, llmGuide, a persona's textsthe changed lines with two kept lines around each change, and … 37 unchanged lines for the rest
a procedure's steps and local factsa line per part, step 'askCity' changed, which opens to its fields written the same way
an added entryfolded as “7 fields”, since it holds the whole resource

The action keeps its own word: an entry is something that happened (added, edited, deleted, renamed, turned on, turned off, created, pushed), and a comparison is a net result (added, removed, changed). The same colour means the same thing in both. A comparison's added or removed resource lists no fields, as version-diff says.

The comparison's way, everywhere (owner's decision). Kept lines say where a change sits, folds keep a long article short, and two separate edits stay two. Removed and added lines alone would read two edits far apart as everything between them rewritten, keep a procedure as JSON, and lose the folds version-diff promises. The web and suwagcms history then write an entry differently, as the CLI does not change.

An entry that changes a procedure reads as parts too (owner's decision): one way of writing, and a step's change can be read in History as well. An entry keeps a part added or deleted with its fields, since the record is all that is left of it.

A version's page

  • Opening: a version's row on Versions opens .../versions/14. Back returns to the list as it was left, with its page.
  • Top: the number as the page's title, the date, the whole digest, the counts of resources and the document as stored.
  • The window: the server works it out. It holds every entry (owner's decision: one rule, and a pushed row beside the edits often explains them) recorded after the previous version's creation, through this version's own created entry; the first version starts from the beginning. It goes by the order entries were recorded, never the clock, so each entry falls in one window at most, and entries after the latest version in none.
  • Compare with…: a select of the bot's other versions. Choosing v12 shows what going from v12 to v14 changes, between the top and the panel, and writes ?compare=12 to the address, so a reload and a sent link keep it. Close removes it. The rules of a comparison are in version-diff.

Contract: the API and the addresses optional

The API's contract is Suwagcms.Server/openapi.json. This adds one read-only route and one field only the web asks for, and changes nothing else.

WhatBehaviour
a version's window, a new route of the form GET /api/bots/{bot}/versions/{number}/windowAnswers the entries of the window, newest first, one page at a time as the History routes do (limit, before, and page, which adds total), with since and incomplete, which says the window opens before history started. A bot or number it does not know is refused, naming the latest. It only reads, so the guard of AC-change-history-5 still holds.
an entry's written, on the History routes and the window'sThe same changes as a comparison writes them, from the same code, answered only when a numbered page is asked for, as total is. The CLI never asks for one, so what suwagcms history --json prints does not change.
GET /api/bots/{bot}/versions/{id}/historyStays as it is: the entries filed under that version, its creation and its pushes, which is what suwagcms history version 14 prints. It is not the window.
the guard that ties routes to commandsNames the window's route with its command, suwagcms versions window, which the Claude plugin's main skill describes: everything in the CMS can be done through them (owner's decision).
/bots/{bot}/versions/{number}, ?compare=New web addresses, the second beside historyPage and the list's page. ?open=, ?from= and ?to= remain as ways in, and lead there.

Decisions optional

  • The entry is part of the save, so a change can never exist unrecorded.
  • History is not built from versions, because several changes fall between two versions, and many never reach one.
  • History starts without saying who made a change (owner's decision): what and when are useful now, and nothing here assumes how identity will work.
  • History is kept for good: it is what README promises and the simplest; a limit can come when a real need shows up.
  • An edit's entry keeps the changed fields only, old and new: it answers what changed, and an unchanged field costs nothing.
  • A save with no real change adds no entry but still moves the revision, as it always has, so an entry's revision can skip a number.
  • The entries of a batch share an id only when one save changed more than one resource; a single entry has nothing to be told apart from.
  • A resource's history opened with --open is the bot's History page narrowed to that resource, the one page that also exists for a deleted resource; the panel beside a resource's form shows the same entries.
  • A version is a kind of its own in history, filed under its number: created with its digest, pushed with its result.
  • The shared module holds no name (owner's decision): it can then sit on any page, and the page says what it is.
  • A window is worked out by the server from the entries of creation, since an entry carries no version number and the browser would otherwise read the whole history to find one.
  • A window has its own route and command rather than a change to the version's history route, because that one answers what suwagcms history version 14 prints, which does not change.
  • An entry's lines reach the screen as a field of their own, written by the comparison's code from the entry's old and new values, so there is no second aligner, and the entry as recorded stays what the CLI prints.
  • A version's created entry ends its own window, so the page shows when it was made and two windows never share an entry.
  • The net comparison stays beside the entries (owner's decision): a change made and restored is two entries and no difference, and only the comparison says what a push really changes.
  • A row's link stays one rule, the resource's History page, also for a version's row: that page lists its creation and its pushes, which its window does not.

Acceptance criteria

  • AC-change-history-1 Adding, editing and deleting a resource of any kind (fact, knowledge base, article, procedure or action) each adds one entry with its time, the action, the revision and the changed fields with old and new values, a secret only as the reference it holds, shown in the resource's History panel and by suwagcms history <kind> <id>.
  • AC-change-history-2 A refused save and a dry run add no entry, and a save with no real change adds none.
  • AC-change-history-3 When an entry cannot be written, the save fails too and the resource stays as it was.

  • AC-change-history-7 An entry of one bot is not listed for another, also when two resources share an id.
  • AC-change-history-8 A resource without entries says no change has been recorded since the date history started.

Criteria after other features

Criteria of this feature that can only be checked once another feature exists. They have no id yet: when that feature is built, its task moves the item into the acceptance criteria above, where it gets the next free id and its guardrail.

  • after cms-identity: each entry says who made the change, a person or an agent, and entries made earlier say this is not known.

Risks and assumptions

Tests that must follow, with their criteria unchanged: AC-version-create-13 (a version read on its page), AC-version-diff-11 and AC-version-export-10 (what --open prints now arrives through a redirect), and AC-change-history-6, already named by pagination.

Deleted text stays in history. Personal data pasted into an article is kept even after a delete, and no route removes it. If a legal request ever demands removal, it will be a task of its own against the database.

Without who, history cannot say whose change it was. Entries made before cms-identity stay without an author for good. Anyone who can reach the CMS can make a change, so an entry proves what happened, not who did it.

History grows with each save and holds old and new values of long articles. Nobody has measured it; reads are paged so that the page stays quick.
